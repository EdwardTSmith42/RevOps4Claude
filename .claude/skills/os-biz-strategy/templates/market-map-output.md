# Market Map — canonical output shape

Use this shape as the default output for `modes/market-map`. Four analytical sections plus strategic recommendations and a positioning hook.

## Shape

```markdown

3. **Expanding Market** — [applicable or not, reasoning]
4. **Red Ocean** — [applicable or not, reasoning]
5. **Saturation & Skepticism** — [applicable or not, reasoning]

**Conclusion:** The market is **between Level X and Level Y** because [specific reasoning grounded in the input]. Messaging implication: [what this means for how the offer should be pitched].
```

## Key rules

- **Work through all five sophistication levels and all five awareness stages explicitly** — don't jump to a conclusion. The rejected levels (with reasoning for why they don't apply) are part of the output's value. This is the discipline that keeps the reading grounded rather than vibes-based.
- **Temperature is a third, orthogonal cut** — don't collapse it into awareness. A Solution-Aware audience can be Cold or Hot, and the two readings inform different parts of the messaging strategy.
- **Every conclusion has a grounded reason.** Thin reasons (*"it seems competitive"*) signal thin analysis. Reasons should cite specific features of the input offer or audience.
- **Counter-positioning requires a named competitive pattern.** *"Unlike most agencies"* is generic. *"Unlike agencies that hand you a plan and a monthly invoice"* is specific and recognizable to the buyer.
- **Unique mechanism has a name.** The name should be 2–4 words, specific, claim-worthy, and avoid over-the-top (no "blockchain," no "neuro-X," no "quantum"). See `references/positioning-concepts.md` on Unlike Statement pattern.
- **Strategic Recommendations are concrete actions, not abstract principles.** "Build trust with case studies" is abstract. "Run a 60-day pilot program with 2 performance-linked milestones" is concrete.
- **Final Positioning Hook is sharable standalone.** Someone reading only the hook should understand the offer's unique angle.

## Grounding check before shipping

1. Is every section anchored in specific input (offer + audience), not generic market platitudes?
2. Are all levels/stages worked through explicitly?
3. Do the Strategic Recommendations name concrete actions?
4. Does the Positioning Hook pass as a real-world marketing headline (specific, recognizable, avoids jargon)?
