# Positioning Concepts

Canonical content lives at `_shared/references/positioning-concepts.md` — shared with `os-offer` and any future content-strategy skill. Full content: USP, NTPV, Unlike Statement, Elevator Pitch structure, Value Articulation Story 7-beat format, Zone of Genius vs. Competence, Non-Genius Work, opening question strategy, silent observation protocol, mirror-their-language discipline, malleability note.

## When to use in biz-strategy modes

- **`modes/positioning`** — primary consumer. structured interview (a menu of question shapes, one at a time) produces a blueprint with all six output sections (Elevator Pitch, Value Breakdown, USP Snapshot, NTPV Messaging, Value Articulation Story, Top 5 Objections).
- **`modes/market-map`** — uses the Unlike Statement pattern in its counter-positioning section. Uses NTPV vocabulary when framing the unique mechanism.
- **`modes/models-method`** — when the model work surfaces positioning material, may reference NTPV and Zone of Genius concepts.
