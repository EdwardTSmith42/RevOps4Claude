# Named Domain Influences — Strategic Marketing & Positioning

Domain context loaded by `biz-strategy` modes that cite named experts. Influences are preserved as **pointers to real bodies of work**, not as personas for the agent to cosplay. Same convention used in voiceprint and audience conversions.

## Direct-response and conversion tradition

- **Eugene Schwartz** — *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966). Source of the 5 Stages of Awareness ladder and the 5 Stages of Market Sophistication. Canon for stage-matched messaging and sophistication-aware market reading.
- **Dan Kennedy** — direct-response tradition, aggressive clarity, specificity-over-fluff copy standards. *No B.S. Marketing Letter*, *The Ultimate Sales Letter*.
- **Russell Brunson** — *DotCom Secrets*, *Expert Secrets*. Source of Market Temperature (Hot/Warm/Cold), Big Domino belief shifts, and the Schwartz-ladder adaptation for funnel design.
- **Alex Hormozi** — *$100M Offers*. Value Equation (Dream × Likelihood / Time × Effort). Grand-slam offer design.
- **Taki Moore** — coach marketing tradition. Mentioned in source prompts' influence rosters. Black Belt and Boardroom systems.
- **Brian Clark** — Copyblogger. Education-first content marketing.
- **Amy Porterfield** — online course and lead magnet strategy for coaches and solopreneurs. Micro-transformation-in-lead-magnet patterns.
- **Marie Forleo** — conversational persuasion, approachable-expert positioning tone.

## Blue Ocean and strategic consulting tradition

- **W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne** — *Blue Ocean Strategy*, INSEAD. Primary source for the Kim/Mauborgne toolkit (ERRC Grid, Strategy Canvas, Six Paths Framework, Three Tiers of Noncustomers, Buyer Utility Map, Price Corridor of the Mass, Adoption Hurdles Map, Tipping Point Leadership + Fair Process).
- **Blue Ocean Strategy Institute (INSEAD)** and **Blue Ocean Global Network (BOGN)** — the institutional knowledge bases extending Kim & Mauborgne's framework.
- **Alexander Osterwalder** — *Business Model Generation*, Strategyzer. Business model canvas, value proposition canvas. Complementary strategic visualization tradition.
- **Mark Johnson** — Innosight, business model innovation. Referenced in Blue Ocean Navigator source.

## Positioning and brand differentiation tradition

- **Donald Miller** — *Building a StoryBrand*. FROM/TO transformation framing, clarity-driven storytelling, customer-as-hero narrative structure. Referenced in the Value Articulation Story 7-beat format.
- **Nancy Duarte** — *Resonate*. Contrast/tension narrative structure — oscillation between what-is and what-could-be.
- **Rosser Reeves** — originator of the USP concept (*Reality in Advertising*).

## Simon Bowen's Models Method tradition

- **Simon Bowen** — *The Models Method*. Visual-communication methodology for consultants, coaches, and course creators. Source of Genius Model, Four Futures, Iceberg, Now-Where-How Tension, Above/Below Line Models. Green Zone vs. Red Zone positioning.

## How to cite influences in mode output

When a mode needs to reference an influence by name (e.g., applying Schwartz's awareness ladder), cite briefly and move on. Do not pad with biography. Value lives in the concept applied to the user's specific situation.

Avoid persona-stacking sentences that list three or four named experts as a synthesis claim — that's residue from the persona-cosplay era. If a concept comes from a tradition, name the tradition briefly, apply the concept, move on.
