# Potential Offers for Solopreneur Coaches Wanting to Reach $100K+

Below is a comprehensive range of potential offers designed for solopreneur coaches wanting to reach $100K+ by creating leveraged, productized programs:

---

## 1. DIY / Self-Study Programs

### A. Signature Online Courses
1. **"6-Figure Coach Blueprint"**
   - A comprehensive A-to-Z course on transitioning from 1:1 coaching to a leveraged group or course model.
   - Includes modules on product creation, pricing, marketing, and tech setup.

2. **"Profitable Productized Coaching Foundations"**
   - Focused specifically on packaging frameworks: how to structure modules, create teaching assets, and standardize deliverables.

3. **"The Automated Client Attraction System"**
   - Teaches coaches how to build funnels and evergreen webinars to generate consistent leads and sales for their group offers.

### B. Mini-Courses or Workshops
1. **"Launch Your Beta Program" Sprint**
   - A short (2–4 week) workshop to validate ideas and launch a beta coaching product quickly.
   - Ideal for coaches who want to test an idea before fully committing.

2. **"Funnels Made Simple for Coaches"**
   - High-level overview of simple sales funnel structures.
   - Could be a lower-priced front-end offer, leading to a larger signature program.

3. **"Tech-Setup 101"**
   - Step-by-step tutorials for choosing and integrating platforms (payment processors, membership sites, email marketing, etc.).

---

## 2. Done-With-You / Group Implementation Programs

### A. Intensive Bootcamps
1. **"Productized Offer Bootcamp"**
   - A 6- to 8-week intensive group program where participants build their entire productized offer from scratch with guided support.
   - Includes weekly coaching calls, templates, feedback sessions.

2. **"Group Coaching Launchpad"**
   - Focused specifically on launching a group coaching program.
   - Participants finalize curriculum, pricing, marketing plan, and enrollment funnel within a set timeframe.

### B. Cohort-Based Masterclasses
1. **"Live 8-Week Cohort: Scale to Six Figures"**
   - Live weekly training plus breakout sessions, action assignments, and peer accountability.
   - Each cohort ends with a "graduation" event showcasing participants' new offers.

2. **"Build Your First Evergreen Webinar Cohort"**
   - Guided process for creating an automated webinar that sells a high-ticket group coaching or course.
   - Includes live funnel reviews and script critiques.

---

## 3. Memberships & Continuity Offers

### A. Ongoing Support Memberships
1. **"Six-Figure Coach Collective"**
   - Monthly membership with ongoing training modules, live Q&A calls, and expert guest speakers.
   - Community-driven focus where members support each other's launches and share best practices.

2. **"Leverage & Launch Membership"**
   - Curated content library on topics like curriculum design, copywriting, and high-conversion marketing.
   - Regular "hot seat" sessions for members to get feedback on their funnel, offer, or marketing.

### B. Small Group Accountability Pods
1. **"Scale Squad Mastermind"**
   - A premium membership where cohorts of 5–7 coaches meet weekly for accountability and masterminding.
   - Includes templates, trackers, and direct feedback from a mentor or facilitator.

2. **"Accountability Accelerator"**
   - Focused on implementing one major growth tactic each month (e.g., webinar funnel, FB ads, lead magnets).
   - Rolling monthly subscription with clear action steps and group momentum.

---

## 4. High-Level Masterminds & Retreats

### A. Premium Mastermind Programs
1. **"Elite 6-Figure Mastermind"**
   - Exclusive community for coaches who already have some revenue momentum but want to break through to $100K+.
   - Emphasis on collaboration, networking, advanced marketing strategies.

2. **"Accelerator Inner Circle"**
   - High-touch program with monthly strategy sessions, VIP events, and advanced trainings on scaling beyond $100K (multi-six or seven figures).
   - Often capped at a certain number for deeper relationships and specialized attention.

### B. In-Person or Virtual Retreats
1. **"Scaling Summit"**
   - 2–3 day event where coaches gather to strategize, plan, and workshop their next product or launch.
   - Could feature guest experts on branding, copy, operations, etc.

2. **"Productized Offer Intensive"**
   - A shorter, high-impact retreat (1–2 days) dedicated entirely to designing, pricing, and mapping out a new productized offer.
   - Live in-person or virtual format with breakouts for personalized feedback.

---

## 5. Done-For-You (DFY) or White-Label Services

### A. Funnel & Tech Setup
1. **"Complete Course Creation & Setup"**
   - Agency-style service: building out the membership site, sales page, automation, and payment gateways.
   - Coaches provide the content. The DFY service handles all tech and design.

2. **"DFY Webinar Funnel"**
   - Team sets up the registration page, email sequence, and evergreen webinar platform so the coach can just show up and teach.

### B. Branding & Curriculum Design
1. **"Curriculum Architecture Package"**
   - Expert instructional designers structure the coach's intellectual property into a cohesive course or group program.
   - May include slide creation, workbook design, branding elements, etc.

2. **"Productized Offer Branding & Launch"**
   - Full-service from concept to launch: naming, visual identity, messaging, funnel build, and marketing plan.
   - Ideal for coaches who want to outsource everything except the core coaching content.

---

## 6. Coaching & Consulting Services

### A. 1:1 Advisory (Premium)
1. **"Private Scale Coaching"**
   - Personalized guidance on everything from offer creation to marketing strategy.
   - Best for coaches who value individualized attention and are willing to pay top dollar.

2. **"VIP Productization Day"**
   - A one-day intensive where the coach and consultant map out the entire group coaching or course strategy in a concentrated session.
   - Ends with a clear step-by-step implementation plan.

### B. Ongoing Consulting Retainers
1. **"Monthly Growth Partnership"**
   - Ideal for coaches who need continuous strategy updates, funnel reviews, team recommendations, etc.
   - The consultant remains on-call for quick pivots and monthly strategy sessions.

2. **"Launch Consultant on Retainer"**
   - Specialized consulting during the prep and execution of a new group program or course launch.
   - Provides direct support for marketing calendar, copy feedback, ad strategy, and post-launch analysis.

---

## 7. Licensing & Certification Programs

1. **"Licensed 6-Figure Framework"**
   - Coaches pay a licensing fee to use a proven "6-Figure" program structure.
   - They get brand assets, marketing materials, and the right to teach the licensed framework under their own brand.

2. **"Certification for Leverage Coaches"**
   - For advanced coaches who want to certify others in a proprietary methodology.
   - Creates a mini-ecosystem of certified practitioners who can teach/coach the method, generating royalties or licensing fees.

---

## 8. Specialized Tools & Resource Bundles

### A. Templates & Swipe Files
1. **"Productized Offer Template Vault"**
   - A collection of slides, worksheet templates, sales page copy outlines, and email sequences.
   - Coaches can quickly create their course or group program assets using plug-and-play templates.

2. **"Done-for-You Funnel Copy Swipe Files"**
   - Pre-written emails, ad copy, landing page templates specifically tailored for group coaching or course launches.

### B. Software + Training Bundles
1. **"All-In-One Launch Toolkit"**
   - Collaboration with a white-labeled software platform (or a curated set of tools) plus a training portal teaching how to use them.
   - Offers a discount or affiliate link to recommended tech solutions.

2. **"Membership Site-in-a-Box"**
   - A proprietary or white-labeled membership platform that includes a quick-start course on setup, design, and marketing.

---

## 9. Hybrid Offers Combining Multiple Elements

1. **"Launch & Scale Accelerator"**
   - Combination of a self-study course, group coaching calls, and some DFY elements (like funnel templates).
   - Tiered pricing depending on the level of done-for-you support.

2. **"Mastermind + Implementation Team"**
   - High-level mastermind with a built-in support team to handle tasks like copywriting, funnel building, or tech support.
   - Coaches in the mastermind each get a set number of "team hours" per month.

3. **"Membership + VIP Days"**
   - A recurring membership (ongoing support) plus a quarterly 1:1 VIP Day for personalized strategy and auditing.
   - Creates steady monthly revenue plus opportunities for higher-level touchpoints.

---

## 10. Alternative / Non-Obvious Offers

1. **Joint Venture "Speed-Dating" or Networking Platform**
   - Coaches pay a fee to access curated partnership opportunities (e.g., cross-promotions, affiliate deals, co-hosted webinars).
   - Helps them expand reach without heavy ad spend.

2. **"Client-Finder Service"**
   - A marketplace or referral network connecting solopreneur coaches with potential leads, charging a monthly subscription or success fee.
   - Not a typical course but a high-value offer for coaches seeking consistent client flow.

3. **"Ready-to-Run Facebook Group Packages"**
   - Done-for-you community-building solution: daily engagement prompts, content calendars, and growth strategies to attract group members who can become coaching clients.
   - Could be combined with training on how to convert members into paying customers.

4. **Media & Publicity Accelerator**
   - A specialized program focusing on PR, podcast appearances, speaking engagements, and press coverage for new group programs.
   - Addresses the desire for broader recognition and brand authority.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Multiple Formats**: From purely digital DIY courses to high-touch masterminds and done-for-you services.
- **Varied Pricing Levels**: Low-ticket mini-courses or templates can lead into higher-ticket group programs or masterminds.
- **Niche Segmentation**: Each offer can be adapted to target specific coaching niches (health, fitness, relationship, business, leadership, etc.).
- **Stacking & Upsells**: Many of these offers can be combined in a value ladder, where a low-cost mini-course naturally feeds into a higher-cost implementation program or mastermind.

By mixing and matching the best-fit elements from each category, you create a unique offer suite that addresses solopreneur coaches' biggest hurdles — allowing them to leverage their expertise without constantly trading time for money.
