# Potential Offers for Mid-Stage Business Owners (Fractional CMO)

For mid-stage business owners like "Mark the Scaling Founder," who needs strategic marketing leadership but isn't ready (or willing) to hire a full-time CMO. These offers focus on delivering high-impact marketing strategy and execution in a fractional capacity, emphasizing trust, expertise, and measurable ROI.

---

## 1. Strategic Assessments & Audits

### A. Marketing Diagnostic Intensive
- **"Growth Gap Analysis"**
  - A one-time deep-dive evaluating Mark's current marketing efforts, metrics, and positioning.
  - Deliverable: A detailed report outlining missed opportunities, quick-win tactics, and a roadmap for scalable growth.

- **"Competitive Positioning Audit"**
  - Compares Mark's brand messaging, website, and marketing assets against top competitors.
  - Helps identify unique selling points (USPs) and critical differentiation strategies.

### B. Marketing Blueprint Workshop
- **"Custom 90-Day Marketing Blueprint"**
  - Half-day or full-day session mapping out specific campaigns, channels, and budget allocations for the next quarter.
  - Ideal for founders who want a strategic plan but aren't ready to commit to a longer engagement.

---

## 2. Fractional CMO Retainers

### A. Ongoing Leadership & Strategy
- **"Fractional CMO Monthly Retainer"**
  - You function as Mark's part-time Chief Marketing Officer, managing overall strategy, overseeing marketing staff or agencies, and reporting results.
  - Typical deliverables include monthly strategy sessions, KPI reviews, team leadership, and campaign optimization.

- **"Quarterly CMO-in-Residence"**
  - A lighter-touch model with intensive involvement each quarter to set strategy, budgets, and campaign goals.
  - Monthly check-ins ensure alignment and course-correct any underperforming initiatives.

### B. Performance-Based Agreements
- **"Revenue Share Fractional CMO"**
  - Low base retainer plus a percentage of revenue growth directly attributable to marketing efforts.
  - Addresses Mark's skepticism by tying compensation to measurable results.

- **"Milestone-Based Fractional CMO"**
  - Retainer fees triggered by hitting agreed-upon metrics (e.g., number of leads, sales conversions, etc.).
  - Reduces perceived risk and builds trust through shared accountability.

---

## 3. Project-Based Offers

### A. Launch & Campaign Management
- **"Product/Service Launch CMO"**
  - Ideal if Mark wants to roll out a new product line or pivot into a new market segment.
  - You handle campaign strategy, team coordination, funnel creation, and KPIs for the launch period.

- **"Paid Ads & Funnel Overhaul"**
  - You audit existing ad campaigns, landing pages, and marketing funnels.
  - Then rebuild or optimize them to improve conversion rates and lower acquisition costs.

### B. Brand Positioning & Messaging
- **"Rebrand & Positioning Project"**
  - Comprehensive project refining brand identity, messaging, and market positioning.
  - Deliverables could include updated brand guidelines, website copy, and a new brand narrative.

- **"Story-Driven Marketing Initiative"**
  - Crafting a cohesive brand story and implementing it across email, social, and PR channels.
  - Showcases Mark's unique values and authority in his market.

---

## 4. Done-For-You Marketing Execution

### A. Turnkey Marketing Department
- **"All-in-One Fractional CMO + Team"**
  - You bring a small team of specialists (copywriters, designers, ad managers) under your leadership.
  - Mark gets an end-to-end marketing solution without hiring multiple agencies or employees.

- **"Omni-Channel Growth Package"**
  - Full-service package covering email marketing, social media strategy, paid ads, and conversion optimization.
  - You deliver monthly performance reports and handle day-to-day management of campaigns.

### B. Tech & Automation Setup
- **"Marketing Ops System Setup"**
  - Implement or optimize marketing automation tools like HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, or Salesforce.
  - Ensure Mark's lead nurture sequences, CRM, and analytics are running seamlessly.

- **"Retargeting & Nurture Campaign Implementation"**
  - Set up and manage retargeting ads and automated email sequences for warm leads.
  - Focus on closing the gap in Mark's pipeline and converting more prospects into buyers.

---

## 5. Workshops & Group Programs

### A. Group Coaching Program (for Mid-Stage Founders)
- **"Scale & Strategize Collective"**
  - A 12-week group coaching program designed for founders at $500K–$5M to implement proven marketing frameworks.
  - Features weekly live calls, templates, and accountability check-ins, led by you as the fractional CMO.

### B. Mastermind-Style Peer Forum
- **"Growth Leaders Marketing Roundtable"**
  - Monthly virtual roundtable for a curated group of CEOs or founders.
  - You facilitate strategic discussions, share new marketing insights, and offer hot seat feedback on participants' marketing plans.

---

## 6. One-Off Intensives

### A. VIP Marketing Strategy Day
- **"CMO-On-Demand Intensive"**
  - A full-day private session (in-person or virtual) dissecting Mark's entire marketing landscape — strategy, campaigns, budget, and team structure.
  - You leave him with a 6- or 12-month roadmap, detailed action steps, and recommended tools or agencies if needed.

### B. Executive Team Alignment Workshop
- **"Marketing Alignment for Leadership Teams"**
  - A half-day workshop to get Mark and his leadership team on the same page regarding marketing goals, messaging, and budget priorities.
  - Reduces internal friction and clarifies roles, so execution is smoother post-workshop.

---

## 7. Consulting + Implementation Hybrids

### A. "Mentor & Manage" Model
- **"Fractional CMO + Marketing Team Coaching"**
  - You mentor Mark's in-house marketing team (if he has junior marketers) while still acting as the strategic lead.
  - Balances team development with expert oversight — helps Mark grow internal capabilities without sacrificing results.

### B. "Audit & Action" Retainer
- **"Quarterly Audit & Sprint"**
  - Each quarter, you perform a deep audit of campaigns, data, and performance metrics.
  - Followed by a 4-week implementation sprint to fix bottlenecks and optimize new opportunities.

---

## 8. Licensing & Intellectual Property

### A. Proprietary Marketing Framework
- **"License the 5-Pillar Growth System"**
  - You provide a branded strategic framework (e.g., 5-step method to predictable leads).
  - Mark's team can implement it in-house, while you offer periodic support or royalties for ongoing use.

### B. White-Label Marketing Playbook
- **"Done-for-You Marketing SOPs"**
  - A library of SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) for everything from ad creation to CRM management.
  - Mark can buy the playbook license and either implement with his team or hire you to oversee execution.

---

## 9. Risk-Reversal & High-Trust Offers

### A. Conditional Guarantees
- **"ROI or Next Quarter's Free"**
  - If Mark doesn't see a predetermined lift in leads or revenue, you continue the next quarter at a reduced rate or no charge.
  - Demonstrates confidence in your approach, easing trust issues from past disappointments.

### B. Pilot Program
- **"60-Day Fractional CMO Test Drive"**
  - A short-term agreement allowing Mark to experience your strategic leadership before committing long-term.
  - Perfect for skeptical founders who need reassurance of tangible progress early on.

---

## 10. Unique/Non-Obvious Add-Ons

1. **"Founders' Vision-Casting Retreat"**
   - A 1–2 day offsite where you help Mark clarify the bigger picture — his company's vision, mission, and brand story — then map marketing to support it.

2. **"Part-Time Board-Level Advisor"**
   - You join Mark's advisory board specifically for marketing oversight and direction.
   - Involves quarterly board meetings, strategic input on major initiatives, and high-level partnership introductions.

3. **"Marketing Partner Network"**
   - A curated network of trusted service providers (designers, copywriters, PR experts) who can fill execution gaps.
   - Mark pays a subscription or fee for direct access and discounted services under your guidance.

---

### Key Takeaways

- **Flexible Engagement Models**: From intensives and audits to full retainer-based fractional CMO roles.
- **Trust-Building Mechanisms**: Performance-based pricing, pilot programs, and clear ROI metrics reduce Mark's skepticism.
- **Scalable Execution**: Offers range from purely strategic to done-for-you, matching the complexity of Mark's needs.
- **Authority Positioning**: Highlighting your expertise and proven frameworks will address Mark's fear of repeating past failures.

---

*Once you've found an offer or offers you'd like to pursue, your next step is to Map Your Market to understand its sophistication level, awareness level, and heat level. All three factors can impact the ultimate offer you want to put out into the world.*
