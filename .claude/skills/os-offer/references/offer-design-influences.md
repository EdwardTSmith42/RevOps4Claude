# Named Domain Influences — Offer

Domain context loaded by `offer` modes that cite named experts. Preserved as **pointers to real bodies of work**, not personas for the agent to cosplay. Same convention used in voiceprint, audience, and biz-strategy conversions.

## Direct-response / conversion tradition

- **Eugene Schwartz** — *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966). Market Sophistication 5-level framework + 5 Stages of Awareness. Canon for stage-matched messaging and market-sophistication reading.
- **Dan Kennedy** — direct-response tradition. Aggressive-clarity, specificity-over-fluff standards. Source of the "discipline over charisma" copywriting school.
- **Russell Brunson** — *DotCom Secrets*, *Expert Secrets*. Vehicle / Unique Mechanism foundations, Big Domino belief concept, hook-story-offer structure, market temperature framing. Primary source for the Vehicle concept documented in `vehicle-theory.md`.
- **Alex Hormozi** — *$100M Offers*. Value Equation (Dream Outcome × Likelihood / Time × Effort — see `../../_shared/references/hormozi-value-equation.md`). Grand-slam offer design. Obstacles-as-solutions framing. Offer-worthy-market four criteria (massive pain + purchasing power + easy to target + growing market).
- **Taki Moore** — coach marketing tradition. Event-based and delivery-model work adjacent to offer design.
- **Brian Clark** — Copyblogger. Education-first content-marketing tradition.
- **Amy Porterfield** — online course / lead magnet strategy for coaches. Micro-transformation-in-lead-magnet patterns.
- **Marie Forleo** — conversational persuasion tradition.

## Dominant Differentiation tradition

- **Todd Brown** — *Dominant Differentiation*. Primary source for the Unique Mechanism playbook (`unique-mechanism-playbook.md`) — excavation, naming, 75/25 campaign architecture, evidence stacking, promise-exposure spectrum, defensibility.
- **Evaldo Albuquerque** — unique mechanism and copywriting tradition. Co-referenced with Brown in multiple sources (Blue Ocean Vehicle Maker).

## Unique mechanism naming tradition

- **John Carlton** — *Simple Writing System*. Name-setting convention — plain, clear, punchy.
- **Frank Kern** — *Mass Conversion*. Another naming convention — mechanism-by-method-package.
- **Jeff Walker** — *Product Launch Formula*. Mechanism-as-system framing.

## Blue Ocean / strategic consulting tradition

- **W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne** — *Blue Ocean Strategy*. Red ocean vs. Blue ocean framing — the underlying theory behind Brunson's "blue vehicle" concept and Brown's Dominant Differentiation. See `../../os-biz-strategy/references/market-frameworks.md` for the full Kim/Mauborgne toolkit.
- **Alexander Osterwalder** — *Business Model Generation*. Adjacent but broader tradition, referenced in biz-strategy.

## Positioning / narrative tradition

- **Donald Miller** — *Building a StoryBrand*. FROM/TO transformation framing, clarity-driven storytelling. Underpins the Value Articulation Story (7-beat format) used in PPP template. See `../../_shared/references/positioning-concepts.md`.
- **Nancy Duarte** — *Resonate*. Contrast/tension narrative structure.
- **Rosser Reeves** — originator of the USP concept.

## Psychology / behavioral

- **Robert Cialdini** — *Influence*. Persuasion principles (scarcity, authority, social proof) that inform Offer Phase pivot structures.
- **Daniel Kahneman** — Type 1 / Type 2 processing. Adjacent to the Models Method psychological foundation.

## How to cite influences in mode output

When a mode needs to reference an influence by name (e.g., *"Applying Hormozi's Value Equation..."*), cite briefly and move on. Do not pad with biography. The user can look up the source. Value lives in the concept applied to their specific situation.

Do not output sentences like *"You synthesize the offer wisdom of Dan Kennedy with the Dominant Differentiation frameworks of Todd Brown and the Blue Ocean strategic thinking of Kim and Mauborgne."* That's residue from the persona-cosplay era. If a concept comes from a tradition, say so briefly, use the concept, move on.
