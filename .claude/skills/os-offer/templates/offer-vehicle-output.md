# Offer Vehicle — canonical output shape

Use this shape for `modes/offer-vehicle`. The template merges Vehicle-Maker AI (Ingenium Version)'s rich five-component structure with the simpler Dream Outcome/Vehicle/Journey/Price spec from the earlier Vehicle-Maker AI variant.

## Shape

```markdown
# [Vehicle Name]

*Optional: 1-paragraph description of what this vehicle is and why it exists.*

## Movement Mechanics

**FROM:**
[Specific current state — struggles, failures, frustrations the target client is experiencing today. Grounded, not generic.]

**TO:**
[Specific desired outcome and the emotions that accompany it. Specific enough that a buyer can picture themselves in the new state.]

**PATH:**
[The exact steps, ordered, by which the vehicle transports clients from FROM to TO. Not a bullet list of features — a directional arrow of transformation.]

---

## Unique Mechanism

**Mechanism name:** [Named in 2-4 words, following the naming discipline in `../references/unique-mechanism-playbook.md`. Credible, concrete, not over-the-top, not benefit-creep.]

**Explanation:**
[1-2 paragraphs on what the mechanism is and why it works. Uses "Because Bridge" logic where relevant — *because we do X, Y happens. Because Y, you get Z*.]

- [Component 1 of the mechanism — specific sub-process or tool]
- [Component 2]
- [Component 3]

*(3-5 components max — fewer is better for memorability. The components together form the spine of the Teaching Narrative in any 75/25 campaign built around this vehicle.)*

---

## Clear Container

**What's Included:**
- [Specific inclusion]
- [Specific inclusion]
- [Specific inclusion]

**What's Excluded:**
- [Specific exclusion — and why]
- [Specific exclusion — and why]

**Engagement:** [How clients interact — 1:1 / small group / cohort / self-study / hybrid]

**Interaction:** [What the interaction looks like in practice — format, cadence, expectations]

**Where it begins:** [Entry point / kick-off]

**Where it ends:** [Exit point / completion criteria]

---

## Journey Milestones

The path from A to B to C to D to E — not just A to B. Prospects need to see causality.

1. **First Win** — [quick, early victory that validates the vehicle is working — should come in the first 1-2 weeks or first session]
2. **[Major Shift 1]** — [description of transformation + timing]
3. **[Major Shift 2]** — [description]
4. **[Final Destination]** — [what "done" looks like]
5. **Evidence of Arrival** — [specific signals the client has reached the destination]

- [Angle 1 — specific way this vehicle stands apart from the alternatives the prospect has tried]

## Key rules

- **Movement Mechanics is the spine.** FROM + TO + PATH must be specific and grounded in the avatar. Vague Movement Mechanics propagates throughout the vehicle and produces a generic output.
- **Be specific. Avoid generic statements.** Source-stated constraint: specificity is what distinguishes a vehicle-grade spec from a brochure.
- **Avoid jargon and cringeworthy bro-speak.** No "unlock your potential," no "level up your game," no "crush it." Plain, specific, honest.
- **Tie every description to target market + desired transformation + unique expertise.** Every element of the vehicle should clearly connect to the avatar's specific needs and the practitioner's specific capability.
- **Prices end in 0, 5, or 9.** Direct-response pricing convention (Vehicle-Maker constraint).
- **Avoid words like "ethical" and "authentic"** — stated constraint (not persuasive or appealing, lands as virtue-signaling).
- **Container has explicit edges.** What's Excluded is as important as What's Included. Vague containers feel open-ended and lower perceived value.
- **Journey has at least 4-5 milestones.** Think A → B → C → D → E, not A → B. Milestones make the path trustworthy and prevent the transformation being left to chance.
- **Mechanism name passes the red-flag filters** (benefit-creep / vagueness / tongue-twister / trademark-SEO clash). See `../references/unique-mechanism-playbook.md` Section 3, Step 7.
- **3-pillar vehicle structure** (per `../references/offer-frameworks.md`) is a strong default for the Unique Mechanism components — 3 components tends to hit the cognitive sweet spot. 2 is thin, 5+ dilutes. Diverge with a reason.

## Grounding check before shipping

1. Can the user point at their avatar and see themselves in the FROM state?
2. Would a competent competitor recognize what makes this vehicle different?
3. Does the mechanism name pass all four red-flag filters?
4. Are milestones specific enough that a client would know when they've hit each one?
5. Is the Differentiated Positioning statement in the Unlike Statement format with a named alternative (not "unlike most coaches")?
6. If Dream Price is included, does it end in 0/5/9?
7. Is the language free of cringe / jargon / bro-speak / "ethical" / "authentic"?
