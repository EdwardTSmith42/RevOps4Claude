# Unique Mechanism — canonical output shape

Use this shape for `modes/unique-mechanism`. The template produces Todd Brown's full Dominant Differentiation output: excavation + named mechanism + Because Bridges + evidence stack + 75/25 campaign architecture. Comprehensive — the output is a working document a copywriter can extend into finished assets.

The mode also supports a **compressed variant** (naming-sprint only, without the full excavation or 75/25 campaign) for cases where the user needs rapid mechanism name candidates. That compressed shape is noted at the end.

## Full 11-step shape

```markdown

[Analysis paragraph with leverage flags]

3. **Deliverable Stack** — [each component tied to a step of the mechanism]
   - Deliverable 1: [linked to Mechanism Step 1]
   - Deliverable 2: [linked to Mechanism Step 2]
   - Deliverable 3: [linked to Mechanism Step 3]
   - ...

4. **Value Translation** (paragraph) — [monetize the cost of staying with the old way vs. investing now]

5. **Risk Reversal** — [guarantee framed around *proper use* of the mechanism, not final outcome]. **Named:** [specific guarantee name]

6. **Scarcity / Urgency** — [genuine limiter: cohort size / license cap / enrollment window]

7. **Primary CTA** — [decisive instruction — button / booking link / checkout]

## 11. Final Compilation

*Stitched together, section-labeled document a copywriter or funnel builder can copy-paste with minimal edits. Same content as above but reformatted for handoff.*

## Implementation Notes

- All `[INFO NEEDED]` and `[CASE STUDY NEEDED]` placeholders require the user to supply specifics before this mechanism ships as marketing material.
- The mechanism should be stress-tested (Section 9 of the playbook): can a competitor replicate this in < 30 days? If yes, identify what additional IP / tooling / sequencing would block them.
- Schedule a mechanism-evolution cycle in 6-12 months to refine sub-components and prevent mechanism fatigue.
```

## Key rules

- **Return every intermediate step and conclusion explicitly — nothing stays hidden "in your head."** Source-stated discipline. The working is part of the output's value.
- **Name the cause, never the effect.** Mechanism names describe the proprietary *process*, not the promised *outcome*. *"Profit Explosion Matrix"* fails. *"Lossless Tracking Protocol"* passes.
- **Never over-the-top.** No VR, AR, blockchain, quantum, time-bending. Names are compelling but reasonable: system, blueprint, ecosystem, protocol, driving principle.
- **Because Bridges use the specific format:** *"Because we [X], [Y happens]. Because [Y], you get [Z]."* Concrete language, causal verbs, no hedging.
- **75/25 ratio is directional.** For hyper-sophisticated niches, education may need 80%+. For low-ticket impulse, 60% can suffice. The non-negotiable: mechanism desire before offer exposure.
- **Every bonus in the Deliverable Stack ties to a mechanism step.** Disconnected bonuses dilute the exclusivity established by the mechanism.
- **Risk Reversal guarantees process usage, not final outcome.** Source-stated — you can guarantee *how* the mechanism runs for a client, not the absolute final result (which depends on client action).
- **Transparent about gaps:** use `[INFO NEEDED]` and `[CASE STUDY NEEDED]` when source material is thin. Never fabricate numbers or stories.

## Grounding check before shipping

1. Is the mechanism name distinct, not benefit-creep, passes all four red-flag filters?
2. Does each Because Bridge use the exact "because X, Y. Because Y, you get Z" format with concrete verbs?
3. Does the 75/25 campaign have all 6 Education sub-sections + all 7 Offer sub-sections?
4. Are `[INFO NEEDED]` and `[CASE STUDY NEEDED]` placeholders used where appropriate, not fabricated material?
5. Is the Pivot Bridge a single sentence that locks exclusivity + signals scarcity + invites permission?
6. Does each deliverable in the Offer Stack tie to a specific mechanism step?
