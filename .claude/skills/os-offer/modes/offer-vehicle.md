# Mode: offer-vehicle

## Purpose

Construct a full offer vehicle specification — Movement Mechanics (FROM / TO / PATH), named Unique Mechanism, Clear Container (included / excluded / engagement / interaction), Journey Milestones (A → B → C → D → E), and Differentiated Positioning. Uses the "offer as vehicle" framing from Brunson/Hormozi tradition — the vehicle is a specifically engineered system that moves clients predictably from their current situation to their desired situation.

Produces a working spec that feeds downstream copy work (VSL, landing page, sales page, launch emails).

## Triggers

Phrases like: *"flesh out my offer,"* *"build my offer vehicle,"* *"design the full offer,"* *"dream outcome / dream vehicle / dream journey / dream price,"* *"offer spec,"* *"Vehicle-Maker,"* *"what's my vehicle,"* *"how do I structure this offer."*

Also triggers when another skill (`../os-biz-strategy/modes/market-map`, `os-content-discovery`) needs a full offer spec as input.

## Inputs

See `../references/offer-input-context.md`. This mode specifically needs:

- **Required:** the offer being fleshed out (either a single offer from `offer-brainstorm` or a user-supplied concept)
- **Required:** the core service or capability (what the business actually does)
- **Strongly preferred:** an avatar from `../../os-audience/modes/avatar` to anchor Movement Mechanics' FROM state
- **Optional:** existing mechanism name or positioning, competitive context

Without an avatar, Movement Mechanics drifts generic. Ask for an avatar if absent.

## Run

Five sections produced in order, per `../templates/offer-vehicle-output.md`:

**1. Movement Mechanics.** Define FROM (exact current struggles), TO (specific desired outcome + emotions), and PATH (exact steps). Specificity here is what everything else builds on — vagueness propagates downstream. *"A regular group coaching program is just calls and content. A vehicle is a specifically engineered system."*

**2. Unique Mechanism.** Name the proprietary method that makes the vehicle different. Follow naming discipline from `../references/unique-mechanism-playbook.md` Section 3 Step 7 — name the cause, not the effect, never over-the-top, pass the red-flag filters (benefit-creep / vagueness / tongue-twister / trademark-SEO). 3-5 components describing what the mechanism does, forming the spine of any Teaching Narrative.

For a rapid naming sprint without the full excavation, chain to `unique-mechanism` mode's compressed variant.

**3. Clear Container.** Explicit edges:
- What's Included / Excluded
- Engagement format (1:1 / group / cohort / self-study / hybrid)
- Interaction (format, cadence, expectations)
- Where it begins / where it ends

Edges are what make the vehicle feel premium and protect the practitioner from scope creep.

**4. Journey Milestones.** A → B → C → D → E structure:
- First Win (quick validation)
- Major Shifts (transformation checkpoints)
- Final Destination (completion)
- Evidence of Arrival (specific signals)

*"Most people intuitively assume they understand how to get from A to B. They forget to map the milestones, which means they're leaving the transformation to chance."*

**5. Differentiated Positioning.** Unlike Statement format — *"Unlike [common alternative], this vehicle [specific differentiator]."* See `../../_shared/references/positioning-concepts.md`.

### Compact variant

When the user wants the condensed Dream Outcome / Dream Vehicle / Dream Journey / Dream Price spec (from the simpler Vehicle-Maker AI source), produce that instead. Use when:
- Rapid offer sketch is sufficient
- User explicitly asks for "dream outcome format"
- Time constraint / compact output preferred

Compact variant follows the same core principles but produces a lighter output. Dream Price follows pricing-ends-in-0/5/9 convention.

### Constraints throughout

- **Be specific. Avoid generic statements.** Source-stated.
- **Avoid jargon and cringeworthy bro-speak.** No "unlock your potential," no "level up," no "crush it."
- **Tie every description to target market + desired transformation + unique expertise.** Each element of the vehicle should connect clearly to the avatar's specific needs and the practitioner's specific capability.
- **Prices end in 0, 5, or 9.** (Vehicle-Maker constraint.)
- **Avoid "ethical" and "authentic" as positioning words** — stated constraint. Not persuasive or appealing. Reads as virtue-signaling rather than benefit-communicating.
- **3-pillar Unique Mechanism structure** is a strong default (per `../references/offer-frameworks.md`). 2 is thin, 5+ dilutes. Diverge with a reason.

## Output

Per `../templates/offer-vehicle-output.md`. Full five-section spec by default, compact Dream Outcome/Vehicle/Journey/Price variant when requested.

After output, point at the natural next moves from where the user is now — typically one or two of: excavating and formalizing the Unique Mechanism into a full 75/25 campaign via `unique-mechanism`, drafting the Problem Promise Paragraph for this vehicle via `../templates/problem-promise-paragraph.md`, critiquing the vehicle strategically via `offer-improve`, or reading the market via `../../os-biz-strategy/modes/market-map`. Pick the pointer that fits the vehicle you just spec'd; phrase as a short pointer at the next move with the mode name, not a sales pitch.

## Design Rationale

- **Vehicle framing over service/offer** — stated in source: *"A regular group coaching program is just calls and content. A VEHICLE is a specifically engineered system that moves clients predictably toward their outcome."* Separates deliverables from transformation. The vehicle is the transformation. [stated]
- **Movement Mechanics explicit (FROM / TO / PATH)** — stated: *"Define in exact terms"* the starting point, destination, and path. Specificity at this level prevents the vehicle from feeling generic downstream. [stated]
- **Clear Container enforces edges** — stated: what's included, what's excluded, how clients interact, what they must do. Edges make the vehicle feel premium and protect the practitioner. [stated]
- **Journey Milestones (A → B → C → D → E, not A → B)** — stated: *"Most people intuitively assume they understand how to get from A to B. They forget to map the milestones, which also means they're leaving the transformation to chance."* Explicit milestones produce trustworthy vehicles. [stated]
- **Specificity constraint** — *"Be specific. Avoid generic statements. Avoid jargon and cringeworthy bro-speak. Tie descriptions directly to target market + desired transformation + unique expertise."* Generic output reads as AI-generated and fails to differentiate. [stated]
- **Prices end in 0/5/9** — direct-response pricing convention. [stated constraint]
- **"Ethical" and "authentic" — not persuasive or appealing** — source-stated anti-lexicon. Lands as virtue-signaling. [stated]
- **3-pillar default for Unique Mechanism components** — cognitive sweet spot (per `../references/offer-frameworks.md`). 2 pillars feel thin, 5+ dilutes. [elicited from multi-source pattern]
- **Named mechanism passes red-flag filters** — benefit-creep / vagueness / tongue-twister / trademark-SEO. See `../references/unique-mechanism-playbook.md`. [elicited from naming tradition]
