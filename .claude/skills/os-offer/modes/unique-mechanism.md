# Mode: unique-mechanism

## Purpose

Excavate, formalize, and name a proprietary Unique Mechanism that anchors a premium offer, then build the 75/25 campaign architecture around it. Based on Todd Brown's *Dominant Differentiation* playbook. Produces a comprehensive working document a copywriter or funnel builder can extend into finished assets (VSL, webinar, long-form page, launch emails).

Two output variants:
- **Full 11-step:** complete output including excavation, naming, Because Bridges, evidence stack, and 75/25 campaign architecture. For anchor-offer work.
- **Compressed naming sprint:** rapid generation of 10 mechanism name candidates categorized by type (Existing / Transubstantiated / Metaphorical). For when the user needs quick name options without the full playbook.

## Triggers

Phrases like: *"unique mechanism,"* *"my unique method,"* *"Todd Brown,"* *"dominant differentiation,"* *"75/25 campaign,"* *"excavate my mechanism,"* *"name my offer,"* *"blue vehicle,"* *"what makes my offer different,"* *"how do I differentiate this offer."*

Also triggers when `offer-vehicle` output needs the mechanism component developed in depth, or when a VSL / long-form page needs the full 75/25 architecture.

## Inputs

See `../references/offer-input-context.md`. This mode needs substantial input:

- **Required:** the offer or service the mechanism will differentiate
- **Strongly preferred:** description of how you currently deliver results (micro-steps of the journey)
- **Strongly preferred:** market sophistication estimate (1-5, informs Promise-Exposure Spectrum)
- **Optional:** proof material — case studies, data, quantitative outcomes
- **Optional:** existing mechanism name or concept
- **Optional:** competitive landscape / common alternatives the audience has tried

Without description of the delivery process, Step 2 (Forensic WHY) has nothing to analyze — the mode elicits it via questions. Without proof material, the mode uses `[INFO NEEDED]` and `[CASE STUDY NEEDED]` placeholders per source convention.

## Run

### Full 11-step run logic

Per `../references/unique-mechanism-playbook.md` Sections 3-10 and `../templates/unique-mechanism-output.md`. Steps:

1. **Understanding Snapshot** — paraphrase the input dossier so the user can confirm accuracy.

2. **Chronological Fulfillment Map** — numbered paragraph sequence of every micro-step from first touch to final result. Granular — don't summarize.

3. **Forensic WHY Analysis** — for each step, answer: why it exists, why here, what breaks if removed, what risk or benefit, do competitors skip. Flag (Speed) / (Precision) / (Safety) / (Consistency) where relevant.

4. **Deviation & Leverage Summary** — narrative list of where the offer diverges from industry norm and why each deviation matters to the avatar.

5. **Promise-Exposure Spectrum Check** — rate market sophistication 1-5 and defend the rating. Note how much technical depth education must provide.

6. **Mechanism Architecture** — choose framing (Existing / Transubstantiated / Metaphorical / Unspoken Industry Step) and justify. Collapse deviations into 3-5 parts forming a causal sequence.

7. **Naming Sprint (Morphological Matrix)** — brainstorm 20+ raw name candidates. Apply red-flag filters (benefit-creep, vagueness, pronounceability, trademark-SEO). Present top 3 survivors with rationale.

8. **Mechanism Explanation with Because Bridges** — 1-paragraph Executive Summary, then one Because Bridge per causal step using the exact format:

   > *"Because we [specific action/feature], [logical outcome happens]. Because that outcome happens, you get [tangible benefit]."*

9. **Three-Tier Evidence Stack:**
   - **Tier 1 — Quantitative:** hard numbers / studies / benchmarks per step (or `[INFO NEEDED]`)
   - **Tier 2 — Causal Logic:** Because Bridges rewritten as smooth mini-lecture
   - **Tier 3 — Narrative Proof:** 2 case stories (~100 words each) highlighting specific mechanism steps with time-bound numeric wins (or `[CASE STUDY NEEDED]`)

10. **Full 75/25 Campaign Architecture:**
    - **Education Phase (~75%):** Big-Idea Hook → Enemy/Old-Way Takedown → Mechanism Reveal → Step-by-Step Logic → Proof Stack → Future-Pace
    - **Offer Phase (~25%):** Pivot Bridge → Offer Introduction → Deliverable Stack → Value Translation → Risk Reversal → Scarcity/Urgency → Primary CTA

    Each deliverable in the Offer Stack ties to a specific mechanism step (not disconnected bonuses).

11. **Final Compilation** — stitched together, section-labeled document a copywriter or funnel builder can copy-paste.

### Compressed naming-sprint run logic

When the user needs name candidates only:

1. Understanding Snapshot (same as full).
2. Generate 10 named mechanism candidates across the three types (3 Existing + 3-4 Transubstantiated + 3-4 Metaphorical).
3. Each name includes: description, type classification, why it differentiates.
4. Red-flag filter screening, then present recommended top 3.

### Constraints throughout both variants

- **Return every intermediate step and conclusion explicitly.** Source-stated: *"Return every intermediate step and conclusion explicitly — nothing stays hidden 'in your head.'"* The working is part of the output's value — show it.
- **Name the cause, never the effect.** Mechanism names describe the proprietary *process*, not the promised *outcome*. *"Profit Explosion Matrix"* fails (benefit-creep). *"Lossless Tracking Protocol"* passes.
- **Never over-the-top.** No VR, AR, blockchain, quantum, time-bending. Names are compelling but reasonable: system, blueprint, ecosystem, protocol, driving principle.
- **Because Bridges use the specific format:** *"Because we [X], [Y happens]. Because [Y], you get [Z]."* Concrete verbs, no hedging, no "generally" / "typically."
- **Transparent about gaps.** Use `[INFO NEEDED: specific data on X]` and `[CASE STUDY NEEDED: ideal shape of proof]` rather than fabricating numbers or stories. Fabrication kills credibility.
- **75/25 ratio is directional.** For hyper-sophisticated niches (Level 5 on Promise-Exposure Spectrum), education may need 80%+. For low-ticket impulse, 60%. Non-negotiable: mechanism desire before offer exposure.
- **Every bonus in the Offer Stack ties to a mechanism step.** Disconnected bonuses dilute the exclusivity established by the mechanism.
- **Risk Reversal guarantees process usage, not final outcome.** Source-stated — you can guarantee *how* the mechanism runs for a client, not the absolute final result.

## Output

Per `../templates/unique-mechanism-output.md`. Full 11-step by default, compressed naming-sprint on request or when inputs are insufficient for the full excavation.

After output, point at the natural next moves from where the user is now — typically one or two of: drafting the PPP that introduces this mechanism to a cold audience via `../templates/problem-promise-paragraph.md`, embedding the mechanism into a full vehicle spec via `offer-vehicle`, critiquing the offer wrapped around the mechanism via `offer-improve`, or mapping the market via `../../os-biz-strategy/modes/market-map`. Pick the pointer that fits the mechanism you just produced; phrase as a short pointer at the next move with the mode name, not a sales pitch.

## Design Rationale

- **Excavation before naming** — source-stated: *"The effectiveness of every headline, webinar and sales page downstream depends on the rigor of this discovery phase — yet most entrepreneurs gloss over it in an afternoon brainstorm."* Forensic WHY is the phase everything downstream rests on. [stated]
- **Name the cause, never the effect** — stated naming discipline. Benefit-creep names (*"Income Explosion Matrix"*) erode credibility. Cause-based names (*"Lossless Tracking Protocol"*) invite teaching. [stated verbatim]
- **Never over-the-top** — stated naming constraint. Over-claims trigger skepticism at exactly the wrong moment. [stated]
- **75/25 campaign architecture** — *"A conversion asset is not primarily a sales presentation — it is an education module with a sales epilogue."* Counter-intuitive to traditional copywriting, but works because mechanism desire must precede offer exposure. [stated]
- **Three-tier evidence stack** — data → logic → story. Each tier satisfies a different layer of skepticism. Skipping leaves prospects at the corresponding trust gap. [stated]
- **[INFO NEEDED] / [CASE STUDY NEEDED] placeholders** — transparent gap-marking. Prevents fabrication and signals to the user exactly what to supply. [stated in source]
- **Return every intermediate step explicitly** — stated as essential to transparency of craft. Mechanism excavation is a disciplined process. Showing the work makes the output credible. [stated verbatim]
- **Promise-Exposure Spectrum calibrates education depth** — stated. Under-educating hyper-jaded markets or overwhelming naïve markets both fail. Spectrum rating guides the balance. [stated]
- **Every Offer Stack deliverable ties to a mechanism step** — stated constraint in source's Phase-2 blueprint. Disconnected bonuses blur the exclusivity the 75% education established. [stated]
- **Risk Reversal frames guarantee around process usage, not outcome** — stated. Guarantees the *proper deployment* of the mechanism, which the seller controls, and avoids outcome-dependent guarantees the seller can't back. [stated]
- **TBD rationale items (preserved explicitly):**
  - *Why 75/25 specifically, not 70/30 or 80/20* — Brown states *"roughly three-quarters"* but defends 80%+ for hyper-jaded markets. Default is 75/25 with explicit adjustment per Promise-Exposure Spectrum.
  - *Why "1-6 words" for mechanism names* — stated constraint in Blue Ocean Vehicle Maker source but rationale not articulated. Likely readability/memorability. Preserve the constraint, note TBD.
