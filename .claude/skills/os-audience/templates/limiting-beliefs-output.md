## Key rules

- **All cells filled.** Every cell has concrete, specific content. No *TBD*, no placeholders, no *depends on context*. A thin cell is an unmet requirement.
- **No fluff, no disclaimers, no commentary between tables.** The tables are the deliverable.
- **Deeper Belief column is the belief-behind-the-belief, not a restatement.** Dig below the surface belief. See `references/buyer-psychology-beliefs.md`.
- **Counter-Frameworks dislodge, not counter.** A counter argues against. A dislodge reframes or replaces. See `references/buyer-psychology-beliefs.md` on the dislodge principle.
- **Strategy column respects reveal-and-restraint.** Explain what/why, withhold how. The prospect should understand *why* the shift is real and possible, and need the paid offer to execute. See `references/buyer-psychology-beliefs.md`.
- **Quantity discipline:** exactly 2 Big Domino beliefs (webinar-scope rationale), 5+ each for Vehicle, Internal, External (minimum, 10–20 is valid when specificity serves).
- **Sales bullet constraints are strict:** ≤18 words, verb-starts-ending-in-"s", "so you can / that lets you / that empowers you to / that allows you to" tail. Don't paraphrase. The cadence is the craft.

## Section order matters

Deconstruction → Sales Bullets → Belief tables. The deconstruction feeds the bullets, and both feed the tables. Producing tables before the deconstruction flattens them into generic objections.
