# Example: "I'm Not A ___, But I ___" Template + Interview Guide

A worked example demonstrating both modes on the same source post. Source post is human-authored LinkedIn-style content about non-traditional credentials.

## Source Post

```
I'm not an academic, but no one ever asked for my degree.

I'm not a native English speaker, but most of my leads are American.

I'm not a certified coach, but people pay me to help them grow.

I'm not an influencer, but my content drives inbound business.

I'm not a copywriter, but I make a living with words.

I'm not employed, but I feel more secure than ever.

We often feel like we need to be qualified, but our definition is off:

We chase certifications that few people ask for.
We pay for slow and pricey traditional education.
We read all the books and implement nothing.

In most cases, you don't need more information.

You need REAL evidence.

And the only way to get it is to get your hands dirty.

Build an undeniable body of work.

It will make you the most qualified person by default.
```

## extract-template Output

```
# The Credential Flip

I'm not a [CREDENTIAL], but [RESULT WITHOUT IT].
I'm not a [TRADITIONAL QUALIFIER], but [REAL-WORLD OUTCOME].
I'm not a [TITLE/ROLE], but [RECOGNITION OR IMPACT].
I'm not a [LABEL], but [EVIDENCE OF VALUE].
I'm not a [SKILL IDENTIFIER], but [OUTCOME].
I'm not [EMPLOYMENT STATUS], but [FREEDOM RESULT].

We often feel like we need to be [COMMON BELIEF], but the definition is off.

We chase [EXPENSIVE OR OUTDATED VALIDATION METHOD].
We pay for [INEFFICIENT SYSTEM].
We consume [INFO-ONLY LEARNING] and implement nothing.

In most cases, you don't need more [SURFACE-LEVEL SOLUTION].

You need [REAL, TANGIBLE VALIDATION].

And the only way to get it is to [ACTION-DRIVEN ADVICE].

Build [A CREDIBLE ASSET].
It will make you [UNQUESTIONABLY VALUABLE OUTCOME].
```

## interview-guide Output

```
# Interview Guide: The Credential Flip

**High Level Objective:** Reveal the nontraditional path to credibility, success, or fulfillment.

## Questions

---

### 1. Let's start here — what *aren't* you "technically" qualified for... but you're doing it anyway?

- Has anyone ever assumed you had a degree, a title, or some fancy certification you *don't* actually have?
- Are there labels that don't really fit, but you get results that speak for themselves?
- Ever feel a little imposter-y even though your work clearly lands?

> Think things like:
> *"I'm not a designer, but I've built websites that convert like crazy."*
> *"I'm not a therapist, but people come to me when they're stuck."*

---

### 2. What results have you created that *should* require credentials... but didn't?

- Who pays you, and what do they trust you to help with?
- Are there results you've gotten that people usually associate with "experts"?
- What do people say that surprises them about what you're able to do?

---

### 3. Did you skip any of the traditional stuff along the way?

- College, certifications, MBAs... Did you bypass any of that?
- Ever spend money or time on something that didn't actually matter in the end?
- What part of the usual "career path" felt too slow, too rigid, or just not for you?

---

### 4. So what *did* you do instead?

- How did you actually get good at what you do?
- What built your credibility, if not a title?
- What's your version of "learning by doing"?

---

### 5. What's your version of a "body of work"?

- If someone asked, "What makes you legit?" — what would you show them?
- What have you made, done, or helped others do that you're super proud of?
- Do you have any content, testimonials, wins, or case studies that back it all up?

---

### 6. What does being "qualified" mean to *you* now?

- How do *you* define credibility today?
- What matters *more* to you than a piece of paper?
- What would you say to someone who feels like they need permission to start?

---

## Interview Summary

1. What titles or credentials do you *not* have?
2. What big wins have you achieved anyway?
3. What do people assume about you that's totally off?
4. What parts of the traditional path just didn't work for you?
5. What'd you do instead that actually got results?
6. What proof do you have now that shows your value?
7. How do you define what "qualified" means?
8. What's one message you'd want to share with someone who's still waiting for permission?
```

## Why this is a strong exemplar

- **Human-authored source** — the original post reads as real-person writing, not LLM cadence.
- **Template preserves rhetorical rhythm** — the six "I'm not a ___, but ___" lines stay parallel. The escalation (certifications → education → books → permission) is preserved through the slot structure.
- **Template transcends niche** — works for coaches, developers, musicians, freelancers — not locked to the original's audience.
- **Interview guide voice matches the job** — casual, warm, specific prompts under each question. The "Think things like..." concrete examples prevent generic answers.
- **Summary is useable at the bottom** — compact 8-item list a ghostwriter can scan during a call.
