# Multi-Layer Analysis Framework

**This set can grow. The *kind* of lens-set is the canonical part; the exact count is not — read this file rather than restating its contents elsewhere.**

A five-layer analytical structure for extracting the highest-leverage intellectual assets from a source and turning them into modular content angles. Used by `os-content-mining/modes/multi-layer-extract.md`.

The layers are orthogonal: each reveals a different kind of angle. Skipping layers produces shallow output. Running all five forces the extraction to surface insights the others would miss.

## Layer 1 — Surface Analysis ("Face Value")

What is the content explicitly saying?

- **Core Argument:** What is the big idea or claim? How is it framed (problem-solution, opportunity, threat)?
- **Actionable Insights:** What is the most "aha" moment or practical takeaway?
- **Opposition / Counterpoints:** What are the strongest critiques or controversies? What clashes with the status quo?
- **Pitfalls / Mistakes:** What is misunderstood or likely to be done wrong?
- **Solutions / Prescriptions:** What steps or solutions are proposed? What's missing?

## Layer 2 — Truth Mapping & Reality Testing

What must be true for the content to hold?

- **Foundation Check:** What hidden assumptions is the content relying on?
- **Evidence Test:** What evidence is used (anecdote, data)? Where is it thin or flawed?
- **Broader Context:** What larger trends or movements does this tie into?
- **Futurecasting:** What happens if this idea spreads? What are next evolutions or problems?

## Layer 3 — Assumption, Bias, & Blind Spot Analysis

What's the content missing?

- **Hidden Beliefs:** What's being taken for granted? What biases shape the perspective?
- **Blind Spots / Gaps:** What is not being said? Who or what is overlooked?
- **Competing Approaches:** What do rivals or skeptics say instead?
- **Flaw Analysis:** Where do solutions fail? What's the sharpest criticism?

## Layer 4 — Consequence & Impact Chains

What happens downstream?

- **Immediate Impact:** What happens first if this idea is applied?
- **Short / Mid-term Effects:** What changes in the next 1–12 months? Secondary effects?
- **Long-term / Ripple Effects:** What are the compounding or cascading outcomes?
- **Macro Industry Evolution:** Could this shift a market or society? Existential risk or opportunity?

## Layer 5 — Narrative & Engagement Amplifiers

What makes it stick?

- **Personal Story Hook:** What anecdote or transformation illustrates the core idea?
- **What If Scenario:** What's the best/worst case if everyone adopted (or ignored) this?
- **Pattern Interrupt:** What contradiction or twist upends assumptions?
- **Empathy / Identity Tie-In:** How does this affect a persona, tribe, or emotional need?

## Usage pattern

For each layer:
1. Force at least one insight to surface, even if speculative. Layer depth over layer quantity. Don't collapse layers.
2. For each surfaced angle, generate at least one content concept labeled with a format from `../../_shared/references/content-format-taxonomy.md`.
3. Keep angles and ideas modular — each should stand alone without reference to other layers or sections.

## Why these five specifically

- Layer 1 handles what any competent reader extracts on a first pass
- Layer 2 tests the logical foundation — where Layer 1 misses the unstated assumptions
- Layer 3 surfaces what's absent — the hardest axis for a source author to see themselves
- Layer 4 projects forward — Layer 1 looks at the claim, Layer 4 looks at its consequences
- Layer 5 answers "why does anyone care" — the affective and narrative dimension

Collapsing any of the five loses a class of angle.

**Malleability note:** reference-grade but malleable. The five layers are one cut at orthogonal analytical axes — the *kind* of lens-set matters more than this exact five. Skills that want a different cut (merging Truth-Mapping and Bias into one "epistemic" layer, splitting Consequence into internal vs. market, etc.) may diverge. Note the reason when they do.
